Common Questions

Is my calculator data private?

GPA and CGPA drafts are stored locally in your browser rather than on a project server.

Are results official?

No. The calculator is a student planning tool. Always verify official grades and CGPA through university systems.

How do Summer sessions work?

In the CGPA calculator, use the Summer button on a semester row to label it as a summer session while preserving regular semester numbering.
