What is Iqra GPA Calculator?

The Iqra GPA Calculator is a premium academic utility designed specifically for students of Iqra University Islamabad. It solves the complexity of manual GPA calculations by providing an automated, error-free system that follows the Official IU Grading Policy for both batch systems (pre-2022 and 2022 onwards).
