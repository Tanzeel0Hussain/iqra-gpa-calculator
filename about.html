What is Iqra GPA Calculator?

The Iqra GPA Calculator is a free, student-built web tool designed specifically for Iqra University students to calculate their semester GPA and cumulative CGPA quickly and accurately.

We use Iqra University's official 4.0 grading scale so you can always trust the results. No login required, no personal data collected — just open and calculate!

  • ✅100% Accurate — Uses IU's official grading system
  • ⚡Instant Results — No loading, no waiting
  • 📱Mobile Friendly — Works on all devices
  • 🔒Privacy First — No data stored or shared
  • 🆓Completely Free — No subscription required
  • 🌐Available 24/7 — Access anytime, anywhere
