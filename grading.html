📋 Grading Scale

Official Iqra University grading system and letter grade equivalents

IU Official Grading System

Iqra University follows a 4.0 GPA scale with the following letter grade conversions

Letter Grade Grade Points Percentage Description
A 4.00 90% – 100% ⭐ Excellent
A- 3.70 85% – 89% ⭐ Excellent
B+ 3.30 80% – 84% 👍 Very Good
B 3.00 75% – 79% 👍 Good
B- 2.70 70% – 74% 👍 Good
C+ 2.30 65% – 69% ✅ Average
C 2.00 60% – 64% ✅ Average
C- 1.70 55% – 59% ✅ Average
D+ 1.30 50% – 54% ⚠️ Below Average
D 1.00 45% – 49% ⚠️ Below Average
F 0.00 Below 45% ❌ Fail
I — — ⏳ Incomplete
W — — 🚫 Withdrawn
📌

Important Notes

  • Minimum passing grade is D (45%)
  • Minimum CGPA to graduate is 2.00
  • Dean's List requires 3.50+ GPA
  • Grade I must be completed within 1 semester
🏆

Honours Classification

  • 3.70–4.00: Summa Cum Laude
  • 3.50–3.69: Magna Cum Laude
  • 3.00–3.49: Cum Laude
  • 2.50–2.99: Good Standing
