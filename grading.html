📋 Grading Scale

Iqra University Islamabad — Official grading system for Old & New students

🎓
Old Students Grading System
Fail below 60% — Only 6 grade categories
Letter Grade Percentage (%) Grade Points Description
A 88% – 100% 4.00 ⭐ Excellent
B+ 81% – 87% 3.50 👍 Very Good
B 74% – 80% 3.00 👍 Good
C+ 67% – 73% 2.50 ✅ Average
C 60% – 66% 2.00 ✅ Passing
F < 60% 0.00 ❌ Fail

⚠️ Old Students — Key Rules

  • Minimum passing marks: 60%
  • Below 60% = F (Fail)
  • Only 6 grade categories (A, B+, B, C+, C, F)
  • Minimum CGPA to graduate: 2.00
🆕
New Students Grading System
Fail below 50% — Full 11-grade scale
Percentage (%) Letter Grade Grade Points Description
90% – 100% A 4.0 ⭐ Excellent
85% – 89% A- 3.7 ⭐ Excellent
80% – 84% B+ 3.3 👍 Very Good
75% – 79% B 3.0 👍 Good
70% – 74% B- 2.7 👍 Good

56% – 59% C- 1.7 ⚠️ Below Average
53% – 55% D 1.0 ⚠️ Below Average
50% – 52% D+ 0.7 ⚠️ Borderline
0% – 49% F 0.0 ❌ Fail

📌 New Students — Key Rules

  • Minimum passing marks: 50%
  • Below 50% = F (Fail)
  • Full 11-grade scale (A down to D+)
  • Minimum CGPA to graduate: 2.00
  • Dean's List: 3.5+ GPA per semester
🏆

Honours Classification

  • 3.70 – 4.00: Summa Cum Laude
  • 3.50 – 3.69: Magna Cum Laude
  • 3.00 – 3.49: Cum Laude
  • 2.50 – 2.99: Good Standing
  • Below 2.00: Academic Probation
