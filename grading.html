📋 Iqra University Grading Reference

Legacy and revised grade-point tables used by the calculators.

Legacy Grading Scheme

Followed through Fall 2024.

GradePercentagePointsRemarks
A88% – 100%4.00Excellent
B+81% – 87%3.50Very Good
B74% – 80%3.00Good
C+67% – 73%2.50Average
C60% – 66%2.00Below Average
FBelow 60%0.00Fail / Course Repeat

Key notes

  • F is awarded below 60%.
  • The undergraduate degree-award requirement is at least 2.00 CGPA under the current IU undergraduate handbook.
  • I, W and K are administrative/course-status grades and do not carry a grade point in this calculator.

Key notes

  • F is awarded below 50%.
  • The handbook notes a 1.70 semester-GPA probation threshold for the revised undergraduate scheme.
  • At least 2.00 CGPA is required for award of an undergraduate degree.
